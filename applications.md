  <p class="kicker">03 / Applications</p>
  <h1>One statistical language.<br><em>Many scientific questions.</em></h1>
  <p>Multiscale methods are especially useful when scientific information is distributed across resolutions, hidden by noise, or expressed through nonstationary behavior.</p>
</section>

<section class="application-cards">
  <article>
    <span>01 / Biomedical signals</span>
    <h2>Finding diagnostic structure in physiological data</h2>
    <p>Wavelet features expose patterns in heart sounds, gait dynamics, eye movements, protein mass spectra, NMR spectra, and other physiological measurements.</p>
    <ul><li>Heart murmur detection</li><li>Parkinson’s gait analysis</li><li>ADHD eye-tracking dynamics</li><li>Cancer-related spectral analysis</li></ul>
  </article>

  <article>
    <span>02 / Agriculture & food systems</span>
    <h2>Learning from distributed and spectral measurements</h2>
    <p>Distributed learning and multiscale spectral analysis support quality assessment, animal monitoring, and data-intensive smart farming.</p>
    <ul><li>Milk quality assessment</li><li>Smart-farming infrastructure</li><li>Animal social networks</li><li>Compressed-domain learning</li></ul>
  </article>

  <article>
    <span>03 / Environment & physical systems</span>
    <h2>Characterizing dynamics that evolve across scales</h2>
    <p>Self-similarity and Hurst analysis provide a statistical language for turbulent, hydrological, climatic, seismic, and structural processes.</p>
    <ul><li>Atmospheric turbulence</li><li>Hydrology and climate</li><li>Seismic measurements</li><li>Structural health monitoring</li></ul>
  </article>

  <article>
    <span>04 / Modern statistical learning</span>
    <h2>Combining interpretable transforms with flexible models</h2>
    <p>Wavelet-aware learning uses multiscale representations as structured input for denoising, detection, forecasting, and scientific discovery.</p>
    <ul><li>Attention-based denoising</li><li>Robust feature engineering</li><li>Time-varying fractal features</li><li>Low-SNR signal recovery</li></ul>
  </article>
