### 2025

1. **An Advanced Self-Similarity Measure: Average of Level-Pairwise Hurst Exponent Estimates (ALPHEE).** Dixon Vimalajeewa, R. J. Hinton, Fabrizio Ruggeri, and Brani Vidakovic. *IEEE Transactions on Biomedical Engineering*, 72(8), 2567–2579.
   <span class="pub-tags">Wavelets · Hurst exponent · Statistical inference</span>

2. **Multiscale Analysis of Heart Sound Signals in the Wavelet Domain for Heart Murmur Detection.** Dixon Vimalajeewa, Chihoon Lee, and Brani Vidakovic. *Scientific Reports*, 15.
   <span class="pub-tags">Heart sounds · Self-similarity · Classification</span>

3. **Ovarian Cancer Diagnostics Using Wavelet Packet Scaling Descriptors.** R. J. Hinton, Jihyun Byun, Dixon Vimalajeewa, and Brani Vidakovic. *Biomedical Signal Processing and Control*, 102.
   <span class="pub-tags">Wavelet packets · Spectrometry · Diagnostics</span>

### 2024

4. **Utilizing Wavelet Transform in the Analysis of Scaling Dynamics for Milk Quality Evaluation.** Devon Maywald and Dixon Vimalajeewa. In *Time Series and Wavelet Analysis*, 261–279.
   <span class="pub-tags">Milk quality · Spectroscopy · Scaling</span>

### 2023

5. **Gamma-Minimax Wavelet Shrinkage for Signals with Low SNR.** Dixon Vimalajeewa, Anirban DasGupta, Fabrizio Ruggeri, and Brani Vidakovic. *The New England Journal of Statistics in Data Science*.
   <span class="pub-tags">Denoising · Decision theory · Low SNR</span>

6. **Analysis and Classification of ¹H-NMR Spectra by Multifractal Analysis.** Jongphil Kim, Hin Kyeol Woo, Dixon Vimalajeewa, and Brani Vidakovic. *PLOS ONE*.
   <span class="pub-tags">Multifractality · NMR · Classification</span>

7. **Parkinson’s Disease Diagnosis With Gait Characteristics Extracted Using Wavelet Transforms.** Dixon Vimalajeewa, Ethan McDonald, Megan Tung, and Brani Vidakovic. *IEEE Journal of Translational Engineering in Health and Medicine*, 11, 271–281.
   <span class="pub-tags">Gait · Parkinson’s disease · Machine learning</span>

8. **Early Detection of Ovarian Cancer by Wavelet Analysis of Protein Mass Spectra.** Dixon Vimalajeewa, Scott Alan Bruce, and Brani Vidakovic. *Statistics in Medicine*.
   <span class="pub-tags">Cancer diagnostics · Wavelets · Spectrometry</span>

### 2022

9. **Wavelet-Based Approach for Diagnosing Attention Deficit Hyperactivity Disorder (ADHD).** Dixon Vimalajeewa, Ethan McDonald, Scott Alan Bruce, and Brani Vidakovic. *Scientific Reports*.
   <span class="pub-tags">Eye tracking · ADHD · Wavelets</span>

10. **Virus Particle Propagation and Infectivity Along the Respiratory Tract and a Case Study for SARS-CoV-2.** Dixon Vimalajeewa, Sasitharan Balasubramaniam, Donagh P. Berry, and Gerald Barry. *Scientific Reports*, 12.
    <span class="pub-tags">Mathematical modeling · Molecular communication</span>

11. **A Service-Based Joint Model Used for Distributed Learning: Application for Smart Agriculture.** Dixon Vimalajeewa, Sasitharan Balasubramaniam, Donagh P. Berry, and Chamil Kulatunga. *IEEE Transactions on Emerging Topics in Computing*, 10(2), 838–854.
    <span class="pub-tags">Distributed learning · Smart agriculture</span>
